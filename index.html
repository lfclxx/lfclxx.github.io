Watch

Watch is a personal desktop tool that lets its owner schedule and publish their own videos to their own TikTok account. It uses TikTok's official Login Kit for authorization and the Content Posting API (Direct Post) to upload videos.

The app runs locally on the owner's machine. It does not collect, store, or share any TikTok data with third parties. Authorization is granted only for the owner's own account.
