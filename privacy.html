Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Scope

Watch is a personal, self-hosted desktop application used only by its owner. This policy describes how the app handles TikTok-related data.

2. What data the app handles

  • TikTok OAuth tokens (access token, refresh token, open_id, display name) — obtained when the owner authorizes the app via TikTok Login Kit.
  • Video files and captions — selected by the owner for publishing to their own account.
  • Publish records — timestamps and TikTok publish IDs, kept locally for the owner's history.

3. Where data is stored

All data is stored locally on the owner's own machine in the app's data folder. Nothing is uploaded to any third-party server other than TikTok's official API endpoints.

4. What is NOT done

  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are used.
  • No data from any TikTok account other than the owner's own authorized account is accessed.

5. Token handling

OAuth refresh tokens are stored locally and used solely to keep the owner's own publishing session alive. Revoking the app's authorization in TikTok profile settings deletes the app's access immediately; the locally stored tokens then become void.
