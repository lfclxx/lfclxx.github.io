Terms of Service

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. What Watch is

Watch is a personal, self-hosted desktop application. It allows its single owner to schedule and publish videos that they created and own to their own TikTok account, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API (Direct Post).

2. Who may use it

Watch is a private tool for its owner only. No third-party users are onboarded, and the app is not distributed to the public.

3. Acceptable use

  • The owner only publishes content that they created and have the rights to publish.
  • The owner complies with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service at all times.
  • The app must not be used to post content on behalf of other people or accounts.

4. Authorization & revocation

The app acts only with the explicit TikTok OAuth authorization of its owner. The owner can revoke authorization at any time from their TikTok profile settings (Security & permissions), after which the app immediately loses all access.

5. No warranty

The app is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. Publishing may fail due to TikTok API quotas, review status, or network conditions.
